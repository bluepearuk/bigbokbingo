    About Big Book Bingo

    Built by a recovering addict as a free study aid. This is a not-for-profit project, with no adverts, no charges and no sign-ups. If anything here is incorrect, please let me know.

    Not affiliated with AA. Big Book Bingo is not affiliated with, approved by or endorsed by Alcoholics Anonymous, Alcoholics Anonymous World Services, Inc. or Alcoholics Anonymous Great Britain. It is not AA conference-approved literature.

    Trademarks. "Alcoholics Anonymous", "AA" and "the Big Book" are trademarks of Alcoholics Anonymous World Services, Inc. They are used here only to name the book this tool helps you study.

    Copyright. The summaries, reflections, discussion questions and quizzes are original writing for this site, not text from AA literature. Please read the passages themselves in your own copy of the book.

    Not medical or professional advice. This is a study aid. It is not medical, psychological or legal advice. Always speak to your doctor about medication and your health, and use the helplines above if you need support.

    Privacy. This site collects no personal information. There are no accounts, no cookies and no advertising trackers. To see how many people use it, the site counts visits anonymously with GoatCounter, which records things like the page visited, the type of device and the country, but never who you are. Anything you tick or add is saved only in your own browser on this device and is never sent anywhere. Feedback emails are read only by the site's author and are never shared.

    Accuracy. Page numbers follow the 4th edition and may differ by a page or so in other printings. Tradition readings point to Twelve Steps and Twelve Traditions and to Appendix I of the Big Book. Learn topics draw on the Big Book and the other AA books listed in its front pages, so please check dates and details against your own copies.
